## 👋 About Us

**Effiqo AI** builds AI agents that answer calls, capture leads, and help service businesses turn more inquiries into booked work.

Our flagship agent, **ELISE**, is an always-on voice receptionist and revenue agent. She is trained on each client's own knowledge base, so she can answer questions accurately, qualify callers, and book meetings around the clock. No more missed calls, no more slow follow-up.

## 🤖 Meet ELISE

- 📞 **Inbound voice AI receptionist** that picks up every call, 24/7
- 🧠 **Custom knowledge base** built from your services, pricing, policies, and FAQs
- 🎯 **Lead qualification** that identifies buyer intent and captures caller details
- 📅 **Meeting and job booking** straight into your workflow
- 🔁 **Follow-up** so no inquiry slips through the cracks
